MYGRADEBOOK v1.18.1 UPGRADE
===========================

1. Back up your current files and database.
2. Keep your existing htdocs/config/database.php.
3. Upload all other v1.18.1 files/folders directly into htdocs and overwrite.
4. If upgrading from v1.17+ and your activity/submission tables already exist,
   no schema change is required. Optionally import sql/MyGradebook_v1.18.1_Safe_Upgrade.sql.
5. Press Ctrl+F5 after upload.

Teacher sidebar should include:
- Dashboard
- Students
- Subjects
- Grade Entry
- Activities & Submissions
- Top Performers
- Attendance
- Accounts & Passwords
