MyGradebook v1.18
Teacher Submission Viewer

Teachers can now preview student-submitted PDF, image, DOCX, PPTX, TXT and CSV files from Activities & Submissions.

PDF/images: visual browser preview.
DOCX: extracted document text preview.
PPTX: slide-by-slide extracted text preview.
DOC/PPT: download fallback.

No database migration is required from v1.17.
Keep your existing config/database.php when upgrading on InfinityFree.
