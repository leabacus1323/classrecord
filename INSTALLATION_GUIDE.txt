INFINITYFREE UPGRADE
1. Export/backup your live database first.
2. Backup htdocs.
3. Upload this package's application files into htdocs and overwrite matching files.
4. IMPORTANT: keep your live config/database.php if its credentials are already correct. Do not overwrite it with a local/old credential file.
5. IMPORTANT: keep your existing uploads/ folder contents. Merge, do not delete.
6. In phpMyAdmin select your live database and import:
   sql/MyGrades_v1.27.1_Academic_Period.sql
7. Log out and log back in.
8. Hard refresh (Ctrl+F5).
9. Teacher > Subjects > Add Subject should now show Semester and School Year.
