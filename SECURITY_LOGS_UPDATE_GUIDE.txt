BEHAVIOR
- One active login per account.
- A second login is blocked while the current session has activity within the last 2 hours.
- After 2 hours of inactivity, the stale session can be replaced automatically to prevent permanent lockout.
- Teacher > Student Security Logs shows active student sessions, login/logout events, blocked attempts, and output submission events.
- Force Logout immediately invalidates the selected student's active session.
- Password reset or account disable also invalidates the student's active session.
- IP/device/browser indicators are monitoring evidence only and are not, by themselves, proof of cheating.
